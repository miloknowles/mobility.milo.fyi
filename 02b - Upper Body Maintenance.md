> The rationale of this routine is to (1) warm up the shoulders and spine with light movement, then (2) increase range of motion, then (3) do some effortful movements within the new range of motion to teach the body how to use it. The active movements should feel hard and require concentration!
#### Warmup (5 mins)
- Foam rolling (1 minute)
- Side breathing with foam roller (5 deep inhales and full exhales each side)
	- Relax into the foam roller - I'm usually able to get a few satisfying cracks this way.
- Scapula CARs (5 forward, 5 backwards)
- Shoulder CARs (3/side)
- T-spine openers (5/side)
- Scorpion stretches (5/side)
#### Passive Range Training (6 mins)
- Cross body stretch (30 sec / side)
- Wall block hold (2 mins)
	- I don't have a low bar at a good height for this, so I put my hands on a wall instead.
	- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=stXtHIS8fFQ&t=255s
	- At the end, add 3 x 10" PAIL/RAIL
- Weighted thoracic mobility (1 min)
	- Foam roller underneath upper thoracic spine, knees bent. Brace the abs and keep a posterior pelvic tilt to avoid arching your lumbar spine.
- Hanging cobra (2 mins)
	- I don't have a bar to do this... maybe drop it?
	- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=stXtHIS8fFQ&t=255s
#### Active Range Training (3 mins)
- Reverse plank (1 minute)
- Prone swimmers (5 slow and controlled)
	- This should feel hard and uncomfortable! But not painful. I really have to focus to stay at the end range of my shoulders the whole time.
- Stomach overhead press (10 total)
	- Can hold a PVC pipe, bar, or foam roller
	- Pause at end range positions!
	- Keep core engaged, stomach should be off of the ground and ribs should not be flared. This forces the motion to come from the shoulder extension.
#### Swimming Prehab (5 mins)
- Serratus wall rolls/slides (2x10 total)
	- Doing with a foam roller helped impingement a bit!
	- Do 10 rolls from shoulder height to above - these are the hard ones!
	- When I started these I noticed that my right arm (non-dominant) was way weaker and less stable in the overhead position.
- Band external rotations (2x20/side)
	- Progression: use a DB and add weight
	- These were really important for me as a novice swimmer! I only swim freestyle, so I built up a lot of internal rotation stretch without any external rotation strength.
- Band pull apart ISO (1 minute hold)
	- Keep shoulder back and down
- Diagonal band pull parts (2x10/side)
