## Bike
- 70.3 power: 300W (80% of 380 FTP)
- Ironman power: 270W (71% of 380 FTP)
- Those targets require an FTP of about 380W (5 W/kg at 77 kg BW)
- Those power numbers above have to be done while in aero at a goal CdA of about `0.22`.
- Starting baseline: 332W ramp test at a bodyweight of 75 kg, putting my at 4.4 W/kg. To raise that to 380W requires a 14.4% improvement in power. Estimated CdA from the Boston Tri was about 0.25 I believe but very noisy.

## Gaps
- For the swim, the gap is almost entirely technique, followed by top end speed. My current CSS is about 1:21 for 100 yards, and my best 100 yard time trial is about a 1:04/100yd. I need to significantly improve my shoulder mobility and hip extension to reduce drag in the water.
- For the bike, besides the obvious need to just do consistent hard workouts, I need to gain a lot more hip mobility to hold aero comfortably. Limited IR and hip flexion on the left is a recurring problem. A lot of glute work has made me more glute dominant on the bike, but I still get a bit of right hamstring pain there as well.
- For the run, I just need to build up excellent movement mechanics so that I can train consistently. When I ran the 2:45 marathon I was in 2:40 shape but injured. Even so, dropping from a 2:45 to a 2:30 is going from 9.527 mph to 10.48 mph, almost exactly a 10% improvement.
