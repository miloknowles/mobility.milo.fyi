# Business Checkpoints

Goal: test whether the app can become a small, sustainable SaaS (~$120k/yr). Each phase has a pass/fail gate; failing a gate means it stays a personal tool.

## Phase 1 — MVP for myself
**Dec 1, 2026 → Jan 31, 2027 (8 weeks)**

- Build an app that is highly useful to me and helps fix one longstanding mobility issue.
- Day 1: record baseline metrics (range of motion, functional test, pain score, symptom-free run distance) plus photos/video.
- Document progress publicly as early content marketing.
- Open a waitlist landing page during this phase, not after.

**Gate (Jan 31, 2027):** Measurable progress on my own issue, and the app is something I use consistently.

## Phase 2 — First 10 paying power users
**Feb 1 → Mar 31, 2027 (8 weeks)**

- Open up to the waitlist and recruit beyond friends (r/running, triathlon clubs, Strava groups).
- "Would pay" = actually pays (even $5) or pre-orders. Verbal interest doesn't count.

**Gate (Mar 31, 2027):** At least 10 paying users who find it highly useful. Fewer than 10 → clear stop.

## Phase 3 — Scale to 100 paying users
**Apr 1 → Sep 30, 2027 (6 months)**

- Content marketing: Reddit, YouTube, build-in-public posts.
- Track acquisition channel for every user.
- Track retention (target: most users still paying after month 2); consider annual pricing and maintenance plans to offset "recovered and churned."

**Gate (Sep 30, 2027):** At least 100 paying, engaged users → keep going.

## Next milestone (set before Sep 30, 2027)
- Define the next target ahead of time (e.g., 300 users or $5k MRR) with its own deadline.
