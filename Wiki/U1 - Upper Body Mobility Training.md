### Program design

- Superset a flexibility exercise with a strength-at-end-range exercise.
	- Pec stretch + horizontal powell raise
	- Lat stretch + horizontal trap raise
	- ER stretch (stretching internal rotators) + horizontal ER strength
- Always start with the weaker side and match reps on the stronger one.
- Nearly everyone is significantly weaker in ER than IR
- End with hanging

### Flexibility
- Pillar lat stretch: https://www.matthewismith.com/products/mobility-flexibility-toolkit/categories/2975118/posts/2162768893
	- Combine with: Powell Raise
- Loaded pec stretch: https://www.matthewismith.com/products/mobility-flexibility-toolkit/categories/2975118/posts/2154925509
	- Externally rotate the arms. Thumbs point towards the floor. Pecs are internal rotators, so external rotation increases their stretch.
	- Want wrists in line with ears in bottom position. So arms should be angle upwards in a bit of a "Y" position.
	- Slow tempo, pause at the bottom.
	- Superset with Powell raise
- Banded stretch in ER

### Strength
- Trap Raises
	- Start with Trap-3 raises for the lower trapezius. Usually these are not developed enough to maintain shoulder health. Build a foundation of these first to get better long term results. Maintain a ratio of higher Trap-3 volume than Trap-1.
	- Start by doing raises with one arm at a time to deal with asymmetries. 
	- With DB in hand, retract and depress scapula to get set up. Lift arm overhead and up, reset at bottom every time.
	- Start on weaker side! Then match the reps on stronger side.
	- Lower Trap (Trap-3) Raise: Arm at 30-45 degrees in a "Y" position. Scapula starts retracted and depressed.
	- Upper Trap (Trap-1) Raise: Scapula is elevated and protracted.
	- 6-12 reps per side typically
- Prone trap raise
	- Prone or horizontal setup tends to put hardest effort at end range.
- Powell raises
	- Initiate by retracting the scapula
	- Could I do this in a side plank? Yes, can support in a half plank.
	- Suggests a "2020" tempo: 2 eccentric, 2 concentric
- Dumbbell front raise
	- Protract scapula (push shoulder forward) to engage serratus anterior.
	- Slightly wider than shoulder width, stop before arm hits the bottom.
	- Can also bend and cross body
-  Elbow on knee external rotation
	- Tempo = 3112
- Horizontal external rotation

### Hanging 
- https://www.matthewismith.com/products/mobility-flexibility-toolkit/categories/2975128/posts/9913577
	- Pronated grip or supinated grip
	- Passive hanging is helpful if you strong but struggle with flexibility. To focus on flexibility, tuck ribs in more to increase shoulder flexion.
	- Can also do active hangs where you depress your scapula.
	- Progressions: swinging, one arm hands, grip switches, rotating
	- Do this at the end of the workout?
